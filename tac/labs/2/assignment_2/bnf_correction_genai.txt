Here is an analysis of your Backus-Naur Form (BNF) grammar, highlighting critical issues, syntax errors, and structural ambiguities, along with recommended fixes.

---

### Key Issues & Fixes

#### 1. Circular / Left-Recursive Rules (`<recipe_expression>`)

```bnf
<recipe_expression> ::= <recipe_creation> | <recipe_expression> <recipe_add> | <recipe_expression> <recipe_remove>

```

* **Issue:** Direct left-recursion (`<recipe_expression> ::= <recipe_expression> ...`) causes infinite loops in top-down parsers (LL / recursive descent).
* **Fix:** Restructure the rule to be right-recursive or iterative:
```bnf
<recipe_expression> ::= <recipe_creation> <recipe_ops>
<recipe_ops>        ::= <recipe_add> <recipe_ops> | <recipe_remove> <recipe_ops> | epsilon

```



---

#### 2. Endless String Loops via Optional/Epsilon Terminals

```bnf
<string> ::= ""<string_characters>""
<measuring_unit> ::= <lowercase_string>
<lowercase_string> ::= <lowercase_letter> <lowercase_string> | epsilon

```

* **Issue 1:** Extra double quotes `""` around string terminals mean literal double quotes in BNF notation.
* **Issue 2:** `<lowercase_letter>` is never defined in your grammar.
* **Issue 3:** If `<measuring_unit>` matches `epsilon` (empty string), `<ingredient>` can end silently without consuming space or units, leading to ambiguous parsing.
* **Fix:** Define non-empty strings and explicit spaces between ingredient parts:
```bnf
<string>             ::= '"' <string_characters> '"'
<string_characters>  ::= <letter> <string_characters> | epsilon
<measuring_unit>     ::= <lowercase_letter> <measuring_unit_tail>
<measuring_unit_tail>::= <lowercase_letter> <measuring_unit_tail> | epsilon
<lowercase_letter>   ::= "a" | "b" | ... | "z"

```



---

#### 3. Undefined Terminals & Whitespace

* **Missing definitions:** `<lowercase_letter>` was missing completely.
* **Lack of spacing rules:** Terminal tokens inside `<ingredient>` (`<string> <number> <measuring_unit>`) would match concatenated strings like `"flour"500grams` without explicit spaces defined in the grammar or a separate lexer step.

---

#### 4. Structure of `<recipe_content>`

```bnf
<term> ::= <ingredient>
<recipe_content> ::= <term> <tail_list>
<tail_list> ::= <term> <tail_list> | epsilon

```

* **Issue:** While structurally valid for non-empty lists, it lacks delimiter support (like commas `,`) which is common in list syntax (`List([ ... ])`). If ingredients are separated by whitespace, it works as-is.
